Dumbarwadi is a village located in the Vita Taluka of Sangli district in Maharashtra, India. It is administered by a democratically elected Gram Panchayat (village council) under the 73rd Constitutional Amendment. The village economy is primarily agrarian, with sugarcane, soybean, and groundnut being major crops. The Panchayat operates under the Maharashtra Gram Panchayat Act (1958) and provides essential civic services including birth/death registration, sanitation, drinking water, street lighting, and public works.
